**Measures used**

- `[Avg Delay of Late Orders]` – Average days late for late orders (AVERAGEX over FILTER).
- `[Avg Delivery Days]` – Purchase to delivery, days.
- `[Avg Review Score]` – Latest review per order (1-5).
- `[Cancellation Rate]` – Canceled / all orders.
- `[Gauge Max Review]` – Gauge maximum for review score.
- `[Gauge Min]` – Gauge minimum.
- `[Item Revenue]` – Item-grain revenue for product/seller analysis (no fan-out).
- `[Items Sold]` – Order lines of revenue orders.
- `[Late Delivery %]` – Late / delivered orders.
- `[Late Orders]` – Delivered after the estimated date.
- `[On-Time Delivery %]` – Delivered on or before the estimate / delivered orders.
- `[Review Count]` – Review responses.
- `[Seller Avg Review]` – Review score of the orders containing the seller's items (TREATAS bridges items -> orders).
- `[Seller On-Time %]` – On-time rate of the seller's orders.
- `[Target Review]` – Review score target.
