**Measures used**

- `[Anomaly Days]` – Days flagged by >= 2 detectors.
- `[Anomaly Revenue]` – Revenue on anomaly days (blank otherwise, so tables list only anomalies).
- `[Avg Churn Probability]` – Mean P(no purchase in 180 d).
- `[Backtest Actual]` – Actual revenue in backtest windows.
- `[Demand Forecast Units]` – Predicted weekly units.
- `[Forecast Lower 95]` – Lower 95% bound.
- `[Forecast MAPE]` – Mean absolute % error on backtest rows.
- `[Forecast Revenue]` – Predicted daily revenue (selected model).
- `[Forecast Upper 95]` – Upper 95% bound.
- `[High Risk Customers]` – Customers in High / Very High churn bands.
- `[Scored Customers]` – Customers with a churn score.
