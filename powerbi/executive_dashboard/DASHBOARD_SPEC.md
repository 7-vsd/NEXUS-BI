**Measures used**

- `[Average Order Value]` – Revenue / orders.
- `[Gauge Max %]` – Gauge maximum for percentages.
- `[Gauge Min]` – Gauge minimum.
- `[Item Revenue]` – Item-grain revenue for product/seller analysis (no fan-out).
- `[On-Time Delivery %]` – Delivered on or before the estimate / delivered orders.
- `[Revenue PY]` – Revenue in the same period of the previous year.
- `[Revenue YoY %]` – Year-over-year revenue growth.
- `[Target On-Time %]` – On-time delivery target (management threshold).
- `[Total Orders]` – Revenue-recognised orders.
- `[Total Revenue]` – Item price + freight of orders not canceled/unavailable (BRL).
- `[Unique Customers]` – Distinct real customers with a revenue order. FILTER over the fact TABLE (expanded table) restricts DimCustomer; a plain column predicate on FactOrders would not.
