**Measures used**

- `[Active Customers]` – Customers buying in the period (cohort matrix value).
- `[Avg Churn Probability]` – Mean P(no purchase in 180 d).
- `[Estimated CLV]` – Historical CLV estimate = AOV x orders per customer.
- `[High Risk Customers]` – Customers in High / Very High churn bands.
- `[Repeat Customer Rate]` – Share of customers who ordered more than once.
- `[Revenue per Customer]` – Average customer value.
- `[Segment Customers]` – Customers in segment.
- `[Unique Customers]` – Distinct real customers with a revenue order. FILTER over the fact TABLE (expanded table) restricts DimCustomer; a plain column predicate on FactOrders would not.
